# Contributing

CDP is free to use, under the terms in [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE).

Pull requests are welcome, but not every one will be merged - changes need to
fit the protocol's existing conventions and direction. If a PR doesn't get
merged, that's not a judgment on the idea; it may just not be the right fit
for this project specifically.

If CDP doesn't match how you or your team want to work, forking and
customizing it to your own needs is the recommended path. The protocol is
meant to be adapted, not adopted unchanged.

## Reporting issues

Use the issue template to report problems - see it fill in enough detail
(what happened, what you expected, how to reproduce it) that the behavior can
be understood and a fix evaluated.

## Making changes

1. Fork the repository.
2. Make your change on a branch.
3. Open a pull request describing what changed and why.

There's no formal review SLA - PRs are looked at as time allows.
